Only fully remote jobs are listed. Jobs whose posting closed before you applied are removed from the board (the board re-checks open postings every few hours). Jobs flagged Follow up are ones you applied to 10 or more days ago with no reply logged. New LinkedIn alerts are pulled in every few hours during the day; Check for new jobs does it now. Click a job to score it, move it along, or mark it Not interested, Posting expired, or Not selected. For anything else, edit jobs.js or ask an agent working here (the rules are in AGENTS.md).

Score fit with your AI

  1. Click Copy the prompt. It has your resume, about-me.md, and each posting inside it.
  2. Paste it into a new chat in Claude, ChatGPT, or any AI assistant, and send it.
  3. Copy its whole reply (a block starting with {"results") and paste it below.
