Only fully remote jobs are listed. Jobs whose posting expired before you applied are removed from the board. Jobs flagged Follow up are ones you applied to 10 or more days ago with no reply logged. New LinkedIn alerts are pulled in every few hours during the day; Check for new jobs does it now. Click a job to mark it Interested, Not interested, Posting expired, or Not selected (they declined to move you forward) (open the board with Open Job Board.command so the buttons can save). For anything else, edit jobs.js or ask an agent working here (the rules are in AGENTS.md).
