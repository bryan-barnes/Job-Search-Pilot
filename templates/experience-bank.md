# Experience bank

Private. Everything you've done that's true and approved, beyond what fits on a one-page resume.
The master resume is the default one page; this is the warehouse it draws from.

**Rules**
- Entries come from your own words (chat, voice notes, interviews). Date each one.
- Each entry has: what happened, your role, scale or outcome (only numbers you gave), and, once
  you approve it, a **Resume line** (one line, under ~150 characters).
- Application packets may use any entry in letters and answers. They may put an entry on a job's
  resume only if it has an approved Resume line, at most two per resume, and only when the
  posting is about that topic.
- "Ask" marks something an agent should confirm with you before using it.
- **Credit line:** where someone else owned a result, say so in the entry and never cross it.
