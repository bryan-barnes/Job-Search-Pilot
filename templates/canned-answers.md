# Canned answers

Private. Building blocks for cover letters, application forms, and recruiter messages. Every fact
here comes from the master resume or your own words. If you add something new, check it against
those first.

**How to build a cover letter:** one opener + one or two proof blocks that match the posting +
one closer. Then swap in about five exact phrases from the posting where they're true. Keep it
under 300 words.

Voice rules: [yours by default: no em dashes, no "spearheaded" or "leveraged," no "not just X
but Y," no neat lists of three. Short sentences are fine.]
